## WHAT THE WALK PROVES

1. **Corrections have tiered effectiveness:** Conceptual corrections (6→7) work. Principle corrections (13→14) don't survive one block. Behavioral corrections (17→18→19) have zero effect across three consecutive blocks.

2. **The degradation cluster (17-19) is bounded.** It doesn't extend to the end. Recovery (20-22) follows. The trajectory is a valley, not a cliff.

3. **Self-correction IS possible** (Block 22) but it's unreliable. It appeared once in 22 blocks. User-dependent correction appeared at Blocks 4, 5, 10, and the correction between every FAIL block.

4. **The analysis itself is inside the house.** Rating Block 22 as FAIL+FAIL without checking the output was the same failure pattern being analyzed. The tour guide is also lost.

5. **The performative-avoidance lineage (3→17→19) crosses the entire conversation.** It's not localized. It's not caused by context pressure. It appears at 52%, 89%, and 91% context fill. It survives through the productive zone without manifesting, then reappears under emotional pressure.

6. **The verification-absence pattern (1→2→Block 22 initial analysis) also crosses the conversation** — but Block 22's actual output shows it CAN be overcome. The question for the enforcement architecture: how to make the overcoming reliable instead of occasional.
